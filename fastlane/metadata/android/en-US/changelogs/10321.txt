• The app icon no longer carries a notification badge the whole time Reticulum is connected. The always-on "Reticulum service" notification was being counted as something to read, so the badge never cleared and told you nothing. The service notification is still there, as Android requires, but without a badge. A new message or Relay Chat notification still badges the icon, which is what a badge is for.

• Security updates to three libraries the app ships: Bouncy Castle 1.85.2 (the library behind signing and key agreement), commons-compress 1.28.0 (bzip2 for large transfers, now with a patched commons-lang3), and Guava 33.7.1 (pulled in by the emoji picker). None of the fixed vulnerabilities was reachable from this app: they are in certificate, ASN.1, LDAP and temp-file code it never calls. They were in the APK all the same, so they are updated.
