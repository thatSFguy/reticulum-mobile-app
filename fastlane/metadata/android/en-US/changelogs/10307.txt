Relay Chat rooms gain the things direct messages already had: reactions, replies, and copy. Long-press any message in a room for the same quick emoji palette, full emoji picker, Reply and Copy you get in a direct message. Reactions gather on the message as tappable chips showing who reacted and how many — tap one to add or remove your own. Replies quote the message they answer, and a reply whose original has scrolled away still reads as an ordinary message rather than disappearing. Typing an "at" sign now suggests people to mention, drawn from everyone who has spoken in the room, with an option to ask the hub for the full member list when you need someone who has been quiet. All of this uses the published Relay Chat extension format, so replies and reactions interoperate with other clients and hubs that support it, and are simply shown as ordinary messages by ones that do not.
