Fixes a Relay Chat room that showed as connected and joined but never displayed a message. Joining a room by typing its name with a leading hash — which is how every room is displayed, so it is the natural thing to type — created a room the hub knew by a different name: it delivered your messages perfectly, and they were filed where nothing looked for them, splitting the room's history in two. Rooms affected by this are repaired automatically on upgrade. Two indicators that were silently never appearing are also fixed: the unread count on a hub, and the banner telling you which message you are replying to. Rooms gain a "Rejoin room" action for the other cause of a silent room — the hub forgetting your membership after a reconnect — which previously left leaving the room as the only visible option. You can no longer react to your own messages, matching direct messages, and long-pressing a reaction now shows exactly who reacted: Relay Chat attributes each reaction to the identity the hub verified on the link, so it is as trustworthy as who sent the message.
