A rebuilt group-chat experience in the experimental Relay Chat (Rooms) tab — nothing else in the app is affected, and Rooms is still off by default. Tapping the bottom navigation bar no longer throws you out of the room you were reading, a half-written message survives leaving the room or switching tabs, and Back walks up one level instead of leaving the app. You can keep typing while the hub is unreachable, and a message the hub refuses comes back to the box with the reason rather than disappearing. Slash commands now work properly: type "/" for an inline list of the hub's commands; /join, /part, /nick, /me and /clear are handled by the app itself, so joining really does add the room and a nick change applies to your next message; and the hub's answers to /who, /topic and the rest appear in the conversation where you typed them instead of a banner over the app. Rooms also gained unread badges and a "new messages" marker the room opens at, per-room notifications (all messages, mentions only, or muted), highlighting when someone names you by nickname or by part of your identity hash, a member list, day separators, and a timeline that stops yanking you to the newest message while you read history.
