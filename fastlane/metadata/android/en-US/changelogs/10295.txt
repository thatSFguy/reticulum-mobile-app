• Encryption-key continuity across restarts: the app's rotating message key and its rotation schedule now survive app restarts. Previously, relaunching the app twice in a short window could silently break incoming messages from active contacts until your next announce reached them — a brief blip on internet connections, but a long silence over LoRa.
• Retries now pace themselves to the radio: on LoRa the first resend waits 12 s instead of 5 s (a 5 s retry always fired while the delivery confirmation was still on the air, doubling every message), and path lookups get the full 7 s the reference implementation allows before the message goes out.
