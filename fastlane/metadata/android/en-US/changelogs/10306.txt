The Messages tab now works the same way as Rooms. It has an unread count of its own on the bottom bar, which it was missing entirely. Conversations no longer show a "new message" pill while you are sitting in them — a message that arrived as you read it was being counted as unread, which is why the pill appeared and then vanished when you left and came back. Unread counts are also no longer thrown off by a sender whose clock is wrong. Unread badges everywhere now follow one rule: a muted pill for ordinary unread messages, and red only when someone singles you out — a mention of you by name in a group room, or a message from a contact you starred or a conversation you pinned.
