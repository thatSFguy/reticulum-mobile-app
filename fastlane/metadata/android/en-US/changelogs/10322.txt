• New, experimental: connect to a KISS TNC over TCP. The main use is a software modem such as modem73 running on the same phone, driving a ham radio through an AIOC cable, which lets Reticulum messages go out over an ordinary HF/VHF/UHF radio. Turn it on under Settings → Connection → Transports → "KISS TNC over TCP". It connects to 127.0.0.1:8001, modem73's KISS port, unless you change it. Radio settings stay in the modem app, and nothing is sent to the TNC except your packets. Slow radio modes make messages slow, and it doesn't reconnect by itself after the app restarts yet: tap Connect TNC.
