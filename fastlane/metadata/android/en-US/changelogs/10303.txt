Adds an optional anti-spam setting: Settings → Privacy & security → "Require proof-of-work stamps". When you turn it on, your announce tells other people how much CPU work their device must spend before you'll accept a message from them — the mechanism LXMF has for making bulk unsolicited messaging expensive. Off by default, and it changes nothing unless you enable it. A second switch decides whether messages that don't meet your cost are dropped outright or just noted in the diagnostics log and delivered anyway. Note that senders on clients without stamp support can't satisfy any cost, so dropping is the stricter choice.
